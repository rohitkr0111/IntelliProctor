How this demo works
  1. You enable your camera. The browser turns your face position into simple measurements; it does not upload the video.
  2. During calibration, it learns the usual range of those measurements for this session.
  3. Every two seconds, it compares the current measurements to that usual range.
  4. It shows a warning only when a large change continues for several checks. The final report is made only when you press View report.
